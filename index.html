Flagship Project

MYO PO Hub

From manual PO requests to an AI-enabled workflow.
The problem
The purchase-order process at MYO Piccadilly was largely manual. Raising a PO meant creating emails, checking financial coding, updating records by hand and going back and forth with Finance — around 15 minutes per request, with a risk of inconsistent information across emails, spreadsheets and records.
What I built
I designed and built the MYO PO Hub using Microsoft 365 Copilot, Power Apps, SharePoint, Power Automate and a Copilot Agent. It centralises PO requests, automatically stores records in SharePoint, sends structured notifications to Finance, and captures Property Codes, Nominal Codes and L3 Spend Codes consistently. I also built a Copilot Agent, trained on our PO process — currently in testing.

Why This Matters

Efficiency, consistency and scale

The PO Hub isn't just a time saving on one task — it turns a manual, error-prone process into a consistent, centralised workflow that other MYO sites and teams could reuse. It shows how Copilot and the Power Platform work together to solve a real business problem, not just speed up individual tasks.
Early Adopter Insights — What I've Learned

What I've learned

Copilot is most powerful when connected to a workflow.
The biggest opportunity came from combining Copilot with Power Apps, SharePoint and Power Automate — rather than using Copilot only for individual tasks.
Small time savings add up.
Email drafting, information retrieval and summarisation each save minutes, but repeated across the working week they create meaningful cumulative savings.
Copilot can lower the barrier to automation.
Using Copilot as a development partner helped accelerate the creation and troubleshooting of Power Platform solutions — without specialist developer support.
